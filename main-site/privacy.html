Privacy

uwuFix, by Augy Studios (UwU Apps). Last updated 14 November 2026.

uwuFix cleans links. It has no accounts, shows no ads, and keeps your history on your own device. This page says what leaves your device, and why. It covers the website at fixup.uwuapps.org and the uwuFix apps for Android, Windows, macOS and Linux. The Telegram and Discord bots have their own notes in their source code's README files.

What stays on your device

Your history of cleaned links, your look and colour, and which tours you've seen are kept on your device only. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

What leaves your device

When What is sent To
You clean a link (when online) The link, so the server can follow its redirects and read its page's title. It is not kept. uwuFix's server (Vercel), then the link's own site
You use Share history Your history goes straight to the other device you paired with, encrypted. A pairing service only sees a random code and your IP address, to connect the two. Your other device; the PeerJS pairing service (0.peerjs.com) and Google's and Cloudflare's STUN servers, which tell each device its own address
You send a bug report What you type, the contact you give (if any), the pictures you add, and the app's version, your device's model and system version, the page you were on and your language. Augy, on Telegram, through uwuFix's server
You turn on notifications (Android) A Firebase Cloud Messaging token, so Google can deliver them. uwuFix's server keeps no list of devices. Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
The app checks for updates (not the Google Play version) A request for the newest version's number, and the download if you update. uwuFix's server and GitHub
You visit the website Page views, counted by Google Analytics with its cookies. The apps do not use Google Analytics or any other analytics. Google Analytics

What uwuFix doesn't do

  • No ads, and no advertising ID.
  • No accounts, and no database of people or devices.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone, beyond the services above that make the features work.
  • No reading your clipboard in the background: only when you tap paste, or Fix copied link.

Bug reports

A report stays in Augy's Telegram chat so the bug can be fixed. To have one deleted, email augy@augystudios.com with its report number.

Children

uwuFix isn't made for children, and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes and contact

If this changes, this page will say so, with a new date at the top. Questions: augy@augystudios.com.
