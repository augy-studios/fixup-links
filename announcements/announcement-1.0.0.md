✨NEW✨ uwuFix for Android 1.0.0
fixup.uwuapps.org

New:
- Share a link to uwuFix, from any app's Share button or the Direct Share row, and it opens over the app you were in, already cleaned
- Fix link in the text menu: highlight a link anywhere and pick Fix link. In a message you're writing, Replace swaps it for the fixed link right there
- Updates itself, with a bar at the top when a new version is out
- Notifications, only if you want them: the bell in the Edge Tray, for new versions and news
- Everything the website does: trackers stripped, links that preview in Discord and Telegram, redirects followed, history and QR codes

Download the app and view patch notes: github.com/augy-studios/fixup-links/releases/latest
