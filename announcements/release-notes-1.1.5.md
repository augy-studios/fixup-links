Fluent 2 on Windows, macOS Tahoe on a Mac and Frosted Glass on Linux, each with the desktop showing through the window and your accent colour. ## Updating

**Already have uwuFix on your computer?** It updates itself: restart it when it says 1.1.5 is ready, or pick **Check for updates** in the tray.

**Windows**: run the `-setup.exe`. If SmartScreen warns, tap **More info** > **Run anyway**.

**macOS**: open the `.dmg` and drag uwuFix to Applications. The first time, right-click uwuFix > **Open**.

**Linux**: the `.deb` or `.rpm` installs uwuFix in your menu; the AppImage runs from anywhere and updates itself.

Your history and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Look
- **Your desktop's glass**: a new look in **Look** and on the welcome screen, as near to iOS's Liquid Glass as each desktop goes, with your accent colour and the system's own font.
- **Fluent 2 on Windows**: Mica behind the window (your wallpaper, softly blurred and tinted on Windows 11), the page on a layer over it, and Acrylic on the command bar's results.
- **macOS Tahoe on a Mac**: the desktop through the window, the sidebar a rounded pane of glass floating inside its edge, and a capsule search bar.
- **Frosted Glass on Linux**: after Deepin's and COSMIC's, the desktop blurred behind the window where it can (KDE, Deepin and COSMIC), with frosted panes on it.
- **In place of Material You**: on the desktop, glass takes Material You's place beside the UwU Apps look. If you'd picked Material You, you'll find yourself in glass.
