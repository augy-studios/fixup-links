## Updating

**Already have uwuFix on your computer?** It updates itself: restart it when it says 1.1.2 is ready, or pick **Check for updates** in the tray.

**Windows**: run the `-setup.exe`. If SmartScreen warns, tap **More info** > **Run anyway**.

**macOS**: open the `.dmg` and drag uwuFix to Applications. The first time, right-click uwuFix > **Open**.

**Linux**: the `.deb` or `.rpm` installs uwuFix in your menu; the AppImage runs from anywhere and updates itself.

Your history and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Desktop apps
- **Smooth sidebar**: folding the sidebar away and opening it again glides, and the buttons at its top and bottom move with it.
- **History glides away**: putting History away beside the cleaner slides it off to the left, and it slides back in when you show it again.
- **Names on every sidebar button**: with the sidebar open, Show me around, Report a bug, Telegram Bot, Invite Discord Bot and Buy me a coffee have their names beside them, like Fix and History above.
