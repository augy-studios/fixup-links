# Google Play release notes, 1.1.0

uwuFix's first release on Google Play. Paste the block below into the release's **Release notes**
box in Play Console. It stays under Play's 500 characters. The tag must match the store listing's
language; if Play shows it as one it doesn't know, change it to the one Play lists for English.

```text
<en-GB>
• Clean any link: trackers go, and X, Instagram, TikTok and 26 more sites get links that preview
• Share a link to uwuFix, or highlight one and pick Fix link, from any app
• Material You colours, or the UwU Apps look
• Share your history with your other devices, phone to computer, through no server
• A quick tour of each page, and Report a bug with a screenshot
</en-GB>
```
