## Updating

**Already have uwuFix on your computer?** It updates itself: restart it when it says 1.1.7 is ready, or pick **Check for updates** in the tray.

**Windows**: run the `-setup.exe`. If SmartScreen warns, tap **More info** > **Run anyway**.

**macOS**: open the `.dmg` and drag uwuFix to Applications. The first time, right-click uwuFix > **Open**.

**Linux**: the `.deb` or `.rpm` installs uwuFix in your menu; the AppImage runs from anywhere and updates itself.

Your history and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Desktop apps
- **Buttons look clickable**: hovering over a button, a page in the sidebar, a switch or anything else you can click shows the pointing hand. Disabled buttons keep the arrow.
