## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**.

**Already have uwuFix on your computer?** It updates itself: restart it when it says 1.1.3 is ready, or pick **Check for updates** in the tray.

**Windows**: run the `-setup.exe`. If SmartScreen warns, tap **More info** > **Run anyway**.

**macOS**: open the `.dmg` and drag uwuFix to Applications. The first time, right-click uwuFix > **Open**.

**Linux**: the `.deb` or `.rpm` installs uwuFix in your menu; the AppImage runs from anywhere and updates itself.

Your history and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Android app
- **A redrawn Edge Tray**: the arrow tab sits beside the buttons, and the buttons are rounded squares, as on the website.
- **Tucks away smoothly**: tapping the arrow slides the tray off the edge of the screen, leaving only its tab. Tap it again and the buttons roll back in, one after another.
- **More room for the page**: while the tray is out, the page gives up less of the screen beside it, so the cleaner is wider on a phone.
