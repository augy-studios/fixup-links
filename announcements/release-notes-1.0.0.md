The first uwuFix app for Android: share a link to it, or highlight one anywhere, and get it back clean, with embeds that work, without leaving the app you're in.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `uwufix-1.0.0.apk` |

## Installing

**Android**: download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or Files. You only need to do this once. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**.

From then on, the app updates itself: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. There's no need to come back here.

If you see "App not installed", you already have a copy signed with a different key (such as one from PWABuilder). Uninstall uwuFix, then install this one.

Your history and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Android app (new)
- **Share a link to uwuFix**: in any app, tap **Share** and pick uwuFix, from the app list or the Direct Share row above it. The link opens over the app you were in, already cleaned. Copy it, share it on, or press Back to return.
- **Fix link in the text menu**: highlight a link anywhere and pick **Fix link** from the menu that pops up over it. In a message you're writing, **Replace the highlighted link** swaps it for the fixed link right there.
- **Updates itself**: a bar at the top of the app says when a new version is out. **Check for app updates**, the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray, checks whenever you like. **Update** downloads it inside the app and hands it to Android's installer.
- **Notifications, only if you want them**: tap the bell in the Edge Tray to hear about new versions the moment they're out, or news. Nothing is asked until you turn one on.
- **uwuFix links open in the app**: `fixup.uwuapps.org` links, and `uwu://fixup/` links such as `uwu://fixup/?url=https://x.com/...`.
- **Works like an app**: **Share** opens Android's share sheet, **Paste** reads your clipboard, and **Open** hands a fixed link to whichever app takes it, such as X for `fixupx.com`.
- **Status bar** icons follow the app's light or dark mode, not the phone's.

### Everywhere
- **Everything the website does**: trackers stripped, embed-friendly links for Discord and Telegram, redirects followed, history and QR codes. Cleaning works offline after the first launch.
- **Instagram's random trackers**: Instagram's random 4-letter tracking tags are now stripped, whatever letters they use.
- **No header bar**: the logo and title sit at the top of the page on their own, leaving more room for your links.
