## Updating

**Already have uwuFix 1.1.0 on your computer?** It updates itself: restart it when it says 1.1.1 is ready, or pick **Check for updates** in the tray.

**Windows**: run the `-setup.exe`. If SmartScreen warns, tap **More info** > **Run anyway**.

**macOS**: open the `.dmg` and drag uwuFix to Applications. The first time, right-click uwuFix > **Open**.

**Linux**: the `.deb` or `.rpm` installs uwuFix in your menu; the AppImage runs from anywhere and updates itself.

Your history and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Desktop apps
- **Fold the sidebar away**: the button at the top of the sidebar, or Ctrl+B (Cmd+B on a Mac), switches between names and just icons. uwuFix remembers which you like.
- **Put History away**: on a wide window, the « button on History, the button in the title bar, or Ctrl+Shift+H hides History beside the cleaner, giving the cleaner the whole window. History is still a click away in the sidebar.
- **A calmer count**: the number on History is in uwuFix's colour, not red, as it's a count, not a warning.
