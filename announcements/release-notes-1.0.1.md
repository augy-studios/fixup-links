Home screen widgets that fix a link in one tap, and uwu://fix/ links that open uwuFix with a link already cleaned.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `uwufix-1.0.1.apk` |

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**.

Your history and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Android app
- **Home screen widgets**: touch and hold your home screen, tap **Widgets**, and look under uwuFix. Both widgets follow your phone's light or dark mode, and uwuFix only looks at what you've copied when you tap a paste button.
  - **Fix a link**: a bar like a search box. Tap the logo to open uwuFix, or the paste button at its end to clean the link you've copied. It's 4 cells wide and can be narrowed to 2.
  - **Fix copied link**: the paste button on its own, in a single cell.
- **uwu://fix/ links**: put `uwu://fix/` in front of any link, such as `uwu://fix/https://x.com/...`, and opening it brings up uwuFix with that link already cleaned. Handy for shortcuts and automation apps. `uwu://fix/` on its own just opens the app.

### Everywhere
- **Tidier top of the page**: the header bar is gone, and the logo and title sit at the top, level with the Edge Tray.

## What's fixed
- **Shadows behind the Edge Tray**: the buttons' shadows were cut off into a hard-edged box. Now they fade out softly.
