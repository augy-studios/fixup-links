uwuFix is now an app of its own on Android, Windows, macOS and Linux, with Material You, your history shared between your devices, and a quick tour of every page.

## Updating

**Android**: tap **Update** on the bar at the top of the app, or the download-arrow button in the Edge Tray. New to the app? Download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**.

**Windows**: run the `-setup.exe`. If SmartScreen warns, tap **More info** > **Run anyway**. From then on, uwuFix updates itself.

**macOS**: open the `.dmg` and drag uwuFix to Applications. The first time, right-click uwuFix > **Open**. It updates itself after that.

**Linux**: the `.deb` or `.rpm` installs uwuFix in your menu; the AppImage runs from anywhere and updates itself.

Your history and settings stay when you update.

## What's new

### Android app
- **Its own app now**: every screen is drawn by the app itself, not a web page, so it opens faster and works offline from the first tap. Your history and settings come across from 1.0.1 by themselves.
- **Material You**: the app takes its colours from your wallpaper, as Android's own apps do. Prefer the website's look? **Look** in the Edge Tray switches to UwU Apps, with its seven colours.
- **The updates page**: **Check for app updates** opens a page of its own, with what's new in every version, newest first, and a wavy progress bar while it checks and downloads.
- **A smaller, quicker app**: its code is shrunk and optimised as Google Play asks of apps.
- **Notifications, as before**: the bell in the Edge Tray still tells you about new versions the moment they're out.

### Desktop apps (new)
- **uwuFix for Windows, macOS and Linux**: the same app as Android's, with History beside the cleaner on a wide window.
- **Ctrl+K**: paste a link into the bar at the top and press Enter to clean it, or search your history and the app.
- **Fix a link from the tray**: uwuFix waits in the tray (the menu bar on a Mac). **Fix copied link** cleans whatever you've copied.
- **Fix a link widgets**: small windows on your desktop that clean what you paste and copy the result.
- **Make it yours**: pick your look, light or dark, your colour, and whether uwuFix starts when you sign in.
- **Screenshots**: of the window, or the whole page top to bottom, saved to Downloads and copied.
- **Updates itself**, and tells you when a new version is out.

### Everywhere
- **Share history between devices**: in **History**, tap **Share**. One device shows a code and a QR code, the other enters it, and your links go straight from one to the other, through no server. Works between the website, phones and computers, in any mix, on the same Wi-Fi or a hotspot.
- **A tour of each page**: the first time you open a page, it shows you around in a few steps. **Show me around** in the Edge Tray has each tour again.
- **Report a bug**: in the Edge Tray (and the desktop's Help menu). Say what went wrong, add pictures or **Screenshot a page**, which takes the whole page top to bottom, and it reaches Augy on Telegram. You get a report number to quote.
