✨UPDATE✨ uwuFix 1.1.0
fixup.uwuapps.org

New:
- Desktop apps: uwuFix for Windows, macOS and Linux, waiting in your tray with Fix copied link, Ctrl+K to paste and clean, and Fix a link widgets on your desktop
- Share history between devices: History, then Share. One device shows a code (or a QR code), the other enters it, and your links go straight across, through no server. Website, phones and computers, in any mix
- A tour of each page the first time you open it, and Show me around in the Edge Tray for any of them again
- Report a bug, in the Edge Tray: say what went wrong, add a screenshot of the whole page, and it reaches Augy straight away, with a report number to quote

Android app:
- Its own app now, faster, and working offline from the first tap. Your history and settings come across by themselves
- Material You: colours from your wallpaper, or switch to the UwU Apps look in Look
- An updates page with what's new in every version, and wavy progress bars while it checks and downloads
- A smaller, quicker app

Download the apps and view patch notes: github.com/augy-studios/fixup-links/releases/latest
